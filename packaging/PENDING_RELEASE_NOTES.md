### Black Hole

- Add a Simulations section on the mode picker with Black Hole mode: the full PhysicsEngine black hole lab (Kerr and Schwarzschild light, evolving GPU/CPU gas disk, every lab setting, flaring hot-particle glints with amount, brightness, size, variety, duration and whiteness controls, PNG export, and movie export with camera paths) with CAst's Milky Way as the lensed background, adjustable in brightness, contrast, saturation, black level and warmth, plus an optional lensed star overlay with random stars or real Hipparcos catalog stars at their true positions and colors.

### Galaxy Merger

- Add Galaxy Merger mode under Simulations: the full PhysicsEngine galaxy merger lab (two disk galaxies with halos, bulges, cold and hot gas, and star formation integrated as an N-body run, with every lab parameter, resolution presets, run/stop/continue, playback, optical, volume, and particle pictures, camera compositions, optical looks, recordings, and PNG/MP4 export), plus an optional CAst Milky Way and star background (random or real Hipparcos catalog stars) that also appears in saved frames and exports.

### Cluster Flow

- Add Cluster Flow mode under Simulations: the full PhysicsEngine Cosmic Flow lab (gas filaments in a galaxy cluster with dark halos, cooling and black-hole outbursts, or an expanding cosmological volume with live dark matter, with every lab setting, accuracy presets, camera motion and volume exploration, density/speed/temperature/compression views, `.cflow` bake and replay, and PNG/MP4 export); the live simulation pauses while you are in another mode.
